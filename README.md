# Amazon Influencer Playbook

A curated guide for Amazon influencers who want to work better with sellers, written from the seller's side of the table.

Maintained by [Influencer Playbook](https://influencerplaybook.com), a site run by an Amazon seller who works with influencers every week.

## Why this exists

Most advice for Amazon influencers comes from other influencers. This list adds the missing half: what sellers see, what they look for, and why some pitches get a reply while others don't.
